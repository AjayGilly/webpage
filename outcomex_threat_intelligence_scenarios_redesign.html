Coverage depends on the package and statement of work. Scheduled hunting, continuous coverage and escalation windows are not interchangeable. The agreed proposal should clearly state service hours, hunt cadence, notification paths and any applicable SLA before the engagement begins.

Findings are assessed and documented with the available evidence, potential impact and recommended next steps. Outcomex escalates them according to the agreed process.
